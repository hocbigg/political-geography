# Great Courses in Political Geography

This is a curated directory of high-signal university lectures, OpenCourseWare (OCW) modules, and specialized online courses that build deep intuition and strong spatial models for Political Geography.

## Geopolitics, Grand Strategy, and Global Power

- [Geopolitics of Europe (Coursera / Sciences Po / Sylvain Kahn)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/geopolitics-europe)
- [Global Diplomacy: Diplomacy in the Modern World (Coursera / University of London & SOAS / Dr. J. Simon Rofe & Dr. Dan Plesch)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/global-diplomacy)
- [The Changing Global Order (Coursera / Universiteit Leiden / Prof. Madeleine O. Hosli)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/changing-global-order)
- [Political Geography and Critical Geopolitics (YouTube / Virginia Tech / Prof. Gerard Toal)](https://www.youtube.com/@criticalgeopolitics8523)

## Borders, Mobilities, and Territorial Conflict

- [International Migrations: A Global Issue (Coursera / Sciences Po / Prof. Catherine Wihtol de Wenden)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/international-migrations)
- [Europe Without Borders? (edX / The University of Hong Kong / Prof. Stefan Auer)](https://www.edx.org/course/europe-without-borders)
- [Border Realities Lecture Series (UniGR-Center for Border Studies / University of Luxembourg)](https://center-border-studies.uni-gr.eu/en/activities/border-realities)

## Political Ecology, Resources, and Energy Geopolitics

- [Politics and Economics of International Energy (Coursera / Sciences Po / Prof. Giacomo Luciani)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/global-energy)
- [Environmental Security and Sustaining Peace (edX / SDG Academy / UNEP & UNDP)](https://www.edx.org/course/environmental-security-and-sustaining-peace-0)
- [Water Diplomacy (MIT OpenCourseWare / Dr. Animesh Gain & Prof. Lawrence Susskind)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/11-382-water-diplomacy-spring-2021/)
- [Urban Energy Systems and Policy (MIT OpenCourseWare / Prof. David Hsu)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/11-165j-urban-energy-systems-and-policy-fall-2022/)

- [The Crisis of Planetary Urbanization (YouTube / Boitempo / Prof. David Harvey)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JeFkasoah00)
- [The Right to the City and Urban Resistance (YouTube / Boitempo / Prof. David Harvey)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vjyLWMSZ2nY)
