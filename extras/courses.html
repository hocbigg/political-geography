Great Courses in Political Geography

This is a curated directory of high-signal university lectures, OpenCourseWare (OCW) modules, and specialized online courses that build deep intuition and strong spatial models for Political Geography.

Geopolitics, Grand Strategy, and Global Power

  • Geopolitics of Europe (Coursera / Sciences Po / Sylvain Kahn)
  • Global Diplomacy: Diplomacy in the Modern World (Coursera / University of London & SOAS / Dr. J. Simon Rofe & Dr. Dan Plesch)
  • The Changing Global Order (Coursera / Universiteit Leiden / Prof. Madeleine O. Hosli)
  • Political Geography and Critical Geopolitics (YouTube / Virginia Tech / Prof. Gerard Toal)

Borders, Mobilities, and Territorial Conflict

  • International Migrations: A Global Issue (Coursera / Sciences Po / Prof. Catherine Wihtol de Wenden)
  • Europe Without Borders? (edX / The University of Hong Kong / Prof. Stefan Auer)
  • Border Realities Lecture Series (UniGR-Center for Border Studies / University of Luxembourg)

Political Ecology, Resources, and Energy Geopolitics

  • Politics and Economics of International Energy (Coursera / Sciences Po / Prof. Giacomo Luciani)
  • Environmental Security and Sustaining Peace (edX / SDG Academy / UNEP & UNDP)
  • Water Diplomacy (MIT OpenCourseWare / Dr. Animesh Gain & Prof. Lawrence Susskind)
  • Urban Energy Systems and Policy (MIT OpenCourseWare / Prof. David Hsu)

Spatial Theory, Critical Geography, and Urban State Space

  • Reading Marx's Capital with David Harvey (Official Course Repository / CUNY Graduate Center / Prof. David Harvey)
  • The Crisis of Planetary Urbanization (YouTube / Boitempo / Prof. David Harvey)
  • The Right to the City and Urban Resistance (YouTube / Boitempo / Prof. David Harvey)

Spatial Data Science and Computational Cartography

  • Introduction to Spatial Data Science (YouTube / Center for Spatial Data Science, University of Chicago / Prof. Luc Anselin)
  • Gerrymandering and Algorithmic Redistricting (MGGG Redistricting Lab)
