# Summary

The Political Geography curriculum is a **complete education in Political Geography** using online materials.

Political Geography examines the spatial aspects of political processes, including territories, borders, geopolitics, electoral systems, and resource distribution.

## Organization

This repository is organized into 2 main components:

- **Core Curriculum** (this page): the foundational knowledge of the field;
- **[Advanced Topics](advanced_topics.md)**: focused study in specific areas;

**Process:** Learners may work through the curriculum independently or collaboratively, and either sequentially or selectively.

- For simplicity, courses in the Core Curriculum are ordered according to their prerequisites.
- The Core Curriculum provides a shared foundation and is intended to be completed in full.
- Advanced Topics are optional; learners are encouraged to select one area of focus and complete all courses within that topic.

Note: When there are courses or books that don't fit into the curriculum but are otherwise of high quality,
they belong in [extras/courses](extras/courses.md), [extras/readings](extras/readings.md).

# Curriculum

- [Foundations: Spatial Thinking and Political Basics](#foundations-spatial-thinking-and-political-basics)
- [Maps, Space, and Territorial Representation](#maps-space-and-territorial-representation)
- [Core Political Geography](#core-political-geography)
- [Geopolitics and Global Power](#geopolitics-and-global-power)

Study these first (before moving to [Advanced Topics](advanced_topics.md)), in this order:

**Foundations: Spatial Thinking and Political Basics**: Start here. This part teaches you how to think spatially and introduces the fundamental political, theoretical, and geographical concepts you will use throughout the entire curriculum.

**Maps, Space, and Territorial Representation**: Next, learn how maps represent space, how geospatial data functions, and how cartography operates critically as an instrument of political power and territory-making.

**Core Political Geography**: This is the heart of the discipline. Study the essential theories and concepts of states, territory, sovereignty, bordering, nationalism, citizenship, and electoral space.

**Geopolitics and Global Power**: Finish the core by understanding how political geography explains international relations, strategic world orders, and critical perspectives on global power.
